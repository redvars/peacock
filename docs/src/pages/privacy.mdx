# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

This statement covers the Peacock documentation site (peacock.redvars.com), run by {AUTHOR.name}. It explains what the site collects, which third-party services it loads, and the choices you have. The Peacock component library itself collects nothing: it runs in your app and sends no data anywhere.

## 1. What we collect ourselves
This is a static website. There are no accounts, no forms that send data to us, and no database of visitors. We do not ask for or store your name, email address or any other personal details.

The site search runs in your browser against an index that ships with the site, so your search terms are not sent to a server.

## 2. Analytics
We use **Google Analytics** to understand how the documentation is used, such as which pages are read and which browsers and countries visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as `_ga`) and sends Google information about your visit, including the pages you view, your browser and device, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We see these as aggregate reports and do not use them to identify individual visitors.

You can block Google Analytics with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), a content blocker, or your browser's cookie settings. The site works the same without it.

## 3. Advertising
Some documentation pages show ads from **Google AdSense**. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may set cookies and read information about your visit. Depending on your Google settings and your region, the ads may be personalized based on your visits to this and other websites.

You can manage ad personalization in [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services in [Google's partner sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Other third-party services
To display the pages, your browser loads files from these services, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of any web request:

* **jsDelivr** serves the Peacock components, styles and some libraries used by the demos. See the [jsDelivr privacy policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).
* **Google Fonts** serves the fonts. See the [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
* **Unsplash** serves the sample photos on some component demos.
* **The hosting provider** keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and timestamps) for security and to keep the site running.

The GitHub star count and the current version are fetched when the site is built, not when you visit, so viewing them sends nothing to GitHub or npm.

## 5. Settings stored in your browser
The site keeps a few settings in your browser's local storage so they survive the next visit: your light or dark theme, the text direction, and your choices in the theme builder. Some component demos also remember their own state there. These settings never leave your device; clearing your browser's site data removes them.

## 6. External links
The site links to other websites, such as GitHub, npm and the blog's references. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their own policies.

## 7. Changes to this statement
We update this page when the site changes what it collects or which services it uses, and change the "Last Updated" date above.
